## 3. Regional brand context (important)

Derma Skin Clinic appears to be a **multi-country network** (Uganda, Rwanda, Zambia) with shared brand language (“Your journey to a clear skin”), DSC identity, and overlapping service philosophy.

- Uganda site: clinical services + Alion / NeutriHerbs product retail.

**Implication for Rwanda homepage:** Regional service menus are useful evidence of brand capability, but **Rwanda-specific availability must be labelled** unless separately verified in Rwanda channels.

## 6. Brand personality & visual identity

### Personality
- Approachable clinical educator (myth-busting Reels, sunscreen reminders, bilingual EN/Kinyarwanda posts).
- Confidence journey narrative (“From Confusion To Confidence”; skincare journey highlights).
- Regional accessibility + modern clinic environment at Lemigo Hotel.
- Conversion via WhatsApp / call rather than complex booking software.

### Visual assets observed
- **Logo mark:** “DSC” serif monogram + “DERMA SKIN CLINIC” sans wordmark; Instagram avatar uses a multicolour ring around a white circular badge.
- **Clinic exterior:** Modern glass frontage; dimensional white “DSC DERMA SKIN CLINIC” lettering on dark fascia.
- **Uniforms:** White coats embroidered with DSC Derma Skin Clinic.
- **Content pillars:** patient journeys, clinician talking-head Reels, clinic environment, product application, insurance graphics, promotions.
- **Photography tone:** Real African patients and clinicians; clinical but warm; results storytelling without highly polished Western spa stock.

### Colour / type observations
- Existing digital presence leans clean white + charcoal / black signage; social graphics occasionally use warm accent orange in campaigns.
- Uganda Shopify site uses a simple clinical retail aesthetic — **not** a premium international reference for the Rwanda concept.
- For this project, elevate toward **Modern Clinical Luxury** while retaining DSC wordmark logic and recognisable clinic identity (not inventing a conflicting logo system).

## 7. Appointment & communication process

1. Call or WhatsApp the published numbers.  
2. Online consultation offered (Instagram highlight).  
3. Walk-in culture emphasised on Uganda site; Rwanda social content emphasises booking via WhatsApp/call and promotional procedure windows.  
4. Consultation fee (~20k RWF) then personalised plan / possible same-day treatment depending on case.  
5. Insurance: Eden Care, SONARWA, RSSB — patients should still confirm coverage for specific procedures.

**Prototype conversion model:** WhatsApp enquiry form (name, phone, concern, preferred date) → prefilled WhatsApp message. Do **not** imply instant appointment confirmation.

## 8. Differentiators in the Kigali market

- Dedicated dermatology + aesthetic positioning with strong Instagram reach (~40k).
- Insurance partnerships (RSSB + private) — meaningful in Rwanda healthcare access.
- Lemigo Hotel / Gishushu location — premium, accessible commercial district cue.
- Regional brand backing (Uganda/Zambia) and product ecosystem (Alion / NeutriHerbs regionally).
- Online consultation option.
- Active educational content in English and Kinyarwanda.

**Competitors exist** (e.g. Neoderma Clinica and other Kigali dermatology options). Do not borrow their service claims or imagery.

## 9. Public review themes (directional only)

Positive themes: professionalism, effective products, “game changer” aesthetic/dermatology perception, lovely clinic atmosphere.  
Negative themes: waiting times / scheduling communication, product-counter experience, occasional insurance confusion in older reviews.

**Do not publish fabricated testimonials.** Prefer structural trust signals (location, insurance, consultation clarity, clinician-led care) over invented quotes. If using stories later, obtain clinic consent and real patients.

- Lead with Rwanda-verified facts.  
- Frame broader treatment categories as dermatologist-guided care; invite consultation for suitability.  
- No invented credentials, results percentages, or fake reviews.  
- Stock imagery must be temporary, diverse, African-inclusive, and never labelled as Derma patients/staff.  
- Prefer DSC naming + “Your journey to clear skin” brand language already used by the clinic.
