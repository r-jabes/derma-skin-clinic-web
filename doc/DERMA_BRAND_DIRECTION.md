# Derma Skin Clinic Rwanda — Brand & Creative Direction

**Theme:** Modern Clinical Luxury  
**Status:** Applied in homepage prototype

## Positioning

Derma Skin Clinic Rwanda is presented as a **dermatologist-led medical and aesthetic practice in Kigali**, part of a regional Derma Skin Clinic network, with a digital presence that feels international without losing local practicality (WhatsApp, insurance, Lemigo Hotel location).

Promise line (from existing brand language):  
**Your journey to clear skin.**

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ivory | `#F7F5F0` | Page ground |
| Stone | `#E5E0D7` | Soft sections / form surfaces |
| Forest | `#183C35` | Authority, CTAs, headings |
| Sand | `#B69B79` | Accents, eyebrows |
| Charcoal | `#252725` | Body text |

**Typography:** Instrument Serif (display) + Manrope (UI/body).  
**Mark:** DSC monogram + “Derma Skin Clinic / Rwanda” wordmark — aligned with existing clinic signage language.

## Experience principles

1. Brand-first hero (clinic name as primary signal).  
2. Medical credibility before spa glamour.  
3. Honest service framing (Rwanda-verified vs network).  
4. WhatsApp-native conversion.  
5. Insurance as early trust.  
6. Replaceable temporary photography.

## Out of scope for this prototype

- Full multi-page IA  
- Live booking backend  
- Patient results gallery without consent  
- Product e-commerce
